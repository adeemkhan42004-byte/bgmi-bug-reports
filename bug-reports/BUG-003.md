# BGMI-003 — Event Progress Counter Does Not Update After Completing Required Objective

**Severity:** Major
**Priority:** High
**Category:** Events / Rewards
**Type:** Functional
**Status:** Reported / Requires Verification
**Reproducibility:** Intermittent

## Environment

* **Game:** BGMI (Battlegrounds Mobile India)
* **Platform:** Mobile
* **Feature:** Event / Reward system

## Description

The progress counter for an active event may fail to update after the player completes the required collection or objective. As a result, the associated reward may remain unavailable despite the requirement having been completed.

## Preconditions

* An event requiring collection of specific items or cards is active.
* The player is able to complete the relevant event objective.

## Steps to Reproduce

1. Launch BGMI.
2. Open the relevant event.
3. Complete the required collection or objective.
4. Return to the event progress screen.
5. Check the displayed progress counter.
6. Attempt to claim the associated reward.

## Expected Result

The event progress counter should update after the required objective is completed. Once all requirements are fulfilled, the associated reward should become available for claiming.

## Actual Result

The progress counter may remain at an incorrect value even after the required objective has been completed. The associated reward may therefore remain unavailable.

## Reproducibility

**Intermittent — requires verification.**

## Impact

Players may be unable to claim rewards despite completing the required event objective. This can negatively affect event progression and the player's ability to obtain time-limited rewards.

## Severity Rationale

**Major:** The issue can prevent successful progression through an event and may prevent players from receiving an earned reward.

## Priority Rationale

**High:** Event objectives and rewards may be time-limited, making failures particularly disruptive.
